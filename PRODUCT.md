# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Crest88 is for owners and operators of growing small and midsize businesses. They are trying to
move recurring operational work forward without adding the same amount of headcount or losing
control of consequential decisions.

## Product Purpose

Crest88 helps businesses run AI agents inside the work that already operates the company. Agents
handle repeatable work, prepare decisions, and keep activity moving; important actions pause for a
person to approve or deny.

## Positioning

Crest88 combines useful autonomous work with an explicit human decision boundary. It is not a
generic chatbot or an advisory engagement: the product performs operational work while keeping the
owner in control of sensitive actions.

## Operating Context

The product works with the business context, tools, documents, messages, meetings, customers, and
processes a team already relies on. Existing customers enter the authenticated product at
`https://brain.crest88.com/login`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Agents can sort, draft, prepare, summarize, coordinate, and route routine work.
- Consequential outbound actions wait for explicit approval or denial.
- The public site may show sanitized, representative product activity but no tenant data.
- All public lead-generation actions use one contact flow.
- Inquiries route to `hello@crest88.com`; Phillip responds within one business day.
- The public site is static and hosted through GitHub Pages.
- No analytics or marketing cookies are used.

## Brand Commitments

- The product and company name is Crest88.
- The circular animated thinking orb is the global product mark.
- The approved public direction is “Operator”: light, precise, calm, easy to understand, and
  grounded in the real product.
- Human approval and denial must remain visually prominent.
- The voice is plain, specific, and useful; it avoids AI hype.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the product in one viewport.
2. Show the mechanism instead of restating claims.
3. Let agents move routine work; let people decide what matters.
4. Use real customer evidence without embellishment.
5. Make every next action clear and honest.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public site must work with keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, readable
contrast, reduced motion, and responsive layouts from small phones through wide desktop screens.
